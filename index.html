AI Adoption in Europe

How quickly are businesses and people in Europe adopting AI? Official Eurostat figures for 2025, with switchable views.

What the data shows

    Sources: Eurostat, "20% of EU enterprises use AI technologies" (dataset isoc_eb_ai, December 2025) for businesses; Eurostat Statistics Explained on individuals' use of generative AI (datasets isoc_ai_iaiu and isoc_ai_iaiuxr, 2025). Only the figures published in those releases are shown, so country charts list selected countries rather than all member states.
